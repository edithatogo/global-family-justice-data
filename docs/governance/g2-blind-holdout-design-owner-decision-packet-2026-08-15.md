## Option A — 24-edition confirmatory holdout

Recommended. Select one row from each of 24 unseen official aggregate PDFs:
six per frozen stratum, at least 12 jurisdictions, no more than two editions per
jurisdiction and one edition per source series. Pre-rank six stratum-matched
reserves under the frozen deterministic allocation rule.

Trade-offs: strongest proposed transfer evidence and closest fidelity to the
methods panel recommendation, but highest candidate-screening and dual-
extraction burden. Even a perfect result remains bounded PDF reproducibility
evidence, not proof of accuracy or global reliability.

Contingency: if metadata-only screening cannot assemble the exact primary and
reserve scope, stop and return to the owner. Do not shrink, alter strata or
inspect source content to improve eligibility.

## Option B — 12-edition feasibility holdout

Select three editions per same frozen PDF stratum, at least eight jurisdictions,
no more than two editions per jurisdiction and one edition per source series,
with four pre-ranked reserves.

Trade-offs: lower sourcing, rights and operational burden, but feasibility-only
evidence and substantially weaker within-stratum precision. This preserves the
governance panel's resource-conservative recommendation while remaining
compatible with the current PDF-specific atomic schema.

Contingency: if the scope cannot be assembled, stop. Do not reduce it without a
new owner decision.
