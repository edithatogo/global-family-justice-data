## Official route

- Use the official
  [Office of the Chief Justice contact form](https://ocj.judiciary.go.ke/contact-us/)
  and request routing to the Directorate of Planning and Organisational
  Performance, which the Judiciary's published performance material identifies
  as collecting, analysing, and disseminating caseload data and statistics.
- The same official contact page publishes `chiefjustice@court.go.ke` as a
  fallback administrative route.

The source under review is the Judiciary's
[State of the Judiciary and Administration of Justice report](https://judiciary.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/SOJAR-2024-2025-Final-Dec-12.pdf).
Record the form confirmation, sent message, delivery evidence, and any official
ticket or reference number.

## Why an enquiry remains required

The annual report is an official institutional source, but the repository's
assessment remains partial. Family-specific scope, the relationship between
High Court and Magistrates' Court series, coverage across court stations,
historical definition changes, and reuse terms remain unresolved.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — Kenya aggregate family-justice statistics

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are documenting aggregate family-justice statistics reported by
the Kenya Judiciary. Please route this enquiry to the office responsible for
caseload statistics. Could that office confirm:

1. which court and case categories cover divorce, matrimonial property,
   custody, maintenance, adoption, succession, child protection, and
   protection-order matters;
2. which annual measures are available for filed, resolved, pending, and aged
   cases or processing time, including changes to definitions or systems;
3. whether the State of the Judiciary report covers every relevant court
   station and level, and how missing or incomplete returns are identified; and
4. the permitted reuse and attribution terms for report tables and public
   aggregate statistics in public derived observations.

No names, contact details, case identifiers, pleadings, or other identifiable
or case-level information are requested. A response may point to existing
public documentation rather than prepare a bespoke dataset.

Regards,

[repository owner]
