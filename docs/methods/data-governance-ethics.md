# Data governance and ethics

## Public repository boundary

The public repository is for source metadata, non-identifiable aggregate observations, evidence metadata, code, schemas, and documentation. It must not contain names, personal addresses, dates of birth, credentials, protected judgments, sealed records, identifiable case-number linkages, or small cells that create a credible re-identification risk.

## Children and vulnerable parties

Family-justice data may reveal violence, care, migration, disability, health, poverty, and family relationships. The project therefore adopts child-rights, safety, and trauma-informed governance rather than relying only on minimum legal compliance.

Lived-experience participation must be voluntary, remunerated, supported, and structured so that contributors do not need to disclose personal histories publicly.

## Linked and person-level data

Any person-level linkage programme requires a separate secure environment, lawful basis, ethics and data-custodian approvals, data minimisation, access controls, disclosure review, retention rules, and project-specific governance. Only approved aggregate outputs and study metadata belong in this public repository.

## Disclosure control

Public outputs require assessment of:

- small cells and sparse combinations;
- dominance and differencing risks;
- sensitive geographic or demographic breakdowns;
- repeated releases that may enable reconstruction;
- narrative or provenance details that could identify a family;
- source restrictions and court confidentiality rules.

Suppression, aggregation, delayed release, or withdrawal may be required even for nominally public inputs.

## Responsible comparison

- avoid causal claims from descriptive administrative statistics;
- publish uncertainty, missingness, and non-comparability;
- explain legal and procedural context;
- do not treat high filing as automatic failure or high clearance as automatic success;
- do not use throughput as a proxy for safety or wellbeing;
- avoid composite rankings until validity, weighting, and likely consequences have been independently assessed;
- preserve legitimate disagreement and jurisdiction context.

## Rights and security

Public availability does not imply a right to redistribute. Each source requires rights review and a redistribution decision. Security, vulnerability, incident, and takedown processes are defined in `SECURITY.md` and the operations documentation.
