# G1→G2 completion plan

This plan is the execution contract for the dependency-ordered evidence intake in
`config/next_evidence_intake.toml` (`G1-G2-COMPLETION-2026-08-03`). It does not
replace accountable authority, rights decisions, or independent assurance.

## Stages

1. **S1-G1-CLOSE** — bind the owner decision to the current packet digest;
   record charter, decision rights, named roles/deputies, ethics/security
   boundary, and pilot-scope approval. If any field is absent, retain G1 as
   conditional and do not start the real pilot.
2. **S2-G2-SCOPE** — freeze the candidate list, then capture edition receipts,
   complete bytes and SHA-256 hashes, and an explicit rights disposition for
   every candidate. Unknown rights remain metadata-only or quarantined.
3. **S3-G2-ASSURE** — perform independent second extraction and row-level
   comparison, methods adjudication, rights/privacy/security review,
   independent assurance, and the operations rehearsal. Agent panels may
   prepare and validate artifacts but cannot act as accountable authorities.
4. **S4-G3-COVERAGE** — complete reviewed institutional maps, local/regional
   verification, multilingual second review, transparent enquiry closure, and
   preservation/rights audit. Partial or inaccessible coverage remains explicit.

## Controls and contingencies

- Scope changes and outbound contact require explicit owner authorization.
- No observation is promoted from metadata-only/quarantine without qualifying
  source evidence and rights.
- G2 and later gates cannot be accepted by synthetic fixtures, panel advice, or
  local green checks alone.
- Publication remains prohibited until G6 acceptance.

The plan is intentionally fail-closed: unresolved external evidence leaves the
corresponding gate dependency-blocked while repository-owned preparation can
continue.
