Authorize only the `metadata_url_resolver` role to visit the exact 33 official
HTML landing pages in the frozen resolution manifest and identify exact official
PDF URLs plus public terms, rights, privacy and security metadata. It may not
request a PDF/file endpoint, use `HEAD` on one, download, open, render or
transcribe source content.

Trade-off: this is the smallest bounded next step and preserves the existing
44-frame, but it does not reduce the frame's 14-candidate attrition limit or its
weak metadata support for raster/dashboard material.

Contingency: freeze the resolved URL receipt and reassess. If exact locators or
metadata plausibility remain insufficient, return for the Option B expansion;
do not request any candidate PDF.

## Option B — bounded metadata-only expansion to 96

Preserve the exact 44-frame as a baseline, then authorize a new role-separated
metadata registrar to access only search indexes and official HTML catalogue,
landing, terms, rights, privacy and security pages until a maximum 96-candidate
frame is assembled. PDF/file endpoints, downloads, `HEAD` requests, browser
rendering of files, excerpts and target facts remain prohibited.

Trade-off: higher metadata effort and exposure, but substantially reduces the
avoidable risk that structural preflight exhausts the frame before forming the
exact 24+6 scope. The methods panel prefers this option; the operations and
security panels prefer resolving the already frozen 33 pages first.

Contingency: if 96 compliant candidates cannot be assembled under the frozen
search cap without source-file access, stop and return to the owner. Do not
silently accept fewer than the new frozen target.

## Panel recommendation and blocker

Choose Option A, then use Option B as the predeclared contingency. This makes
the least new metadata exposure while producing the exact locator information
needed to decide whether the 44-frame is viable. Regardless of frame size,
source-file authorization must not be executed until the acquisition path has
exact authorization-token and manifest binding, every redirect hop is checked
before connection, public-IP egress is enforced at connection time, private
custody is confined, hostile PDFs are sandboxed, and adversarial negative tests
pass. The current general acquisition client does not yet meet that bar.
