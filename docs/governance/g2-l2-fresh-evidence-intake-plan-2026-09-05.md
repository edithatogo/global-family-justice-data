# G2 L2 fresh M07/M10 evidence intake plan

This is a repository-owned preparation record. It does not claim that the
historical M07 product rehearsal or M10 language review occurred again, and it
does not promote maturity or accept G2.

## M07 — product/accessibility support

Freeze a new evidence bundle before execution containing:

- exact product-build commit and locked toolchain digest;
- two independently produced product/download manifests;
- per-file SHA-256, byte length, media type and deterministic ordering;
- local build and anonymous retrieval procedure, with no credentials or
  personal data;
- repository-native accessibility checks and their machine-readable reports;
- explicit limitation that automation is not lived-experience or manual
  assistive-technology review;
- correction, supersession and restore receipts bound to the same manifest.

Run the build twice from the same frozen inputs, compare manifests exactly,
then perform a separate retrieval verification. Any digest, ordering, schema,
accessibility, prohibited-data or retrieval mismatch stops the intake. Store
only aggregate metadata and receipts in Git; source bytes or restricted test
artifacts remain governed by their existing boundary.

## M10 — international/localisation/sustainability support

Freeze a separate evidence bundle containing:

- exact source-language editions and locators already authorized for review;
- source-language lexical/context observations with provenance;
- authoritative publisher or registry triangulation records;
- explicit distinction between source-defined scope and translated/inferred
  scope;
- the owner resource commitment record and its validity period;
- dissent, uncertainty, missing-language and local-review limitations.

The panel may advise on options and contradictions but cannot claim native,
local-human, institutional or independent specialist assurance. Any unresolved
translation, scope, registry identity or resource-period discrepancy keeps the
dimension in review.

## Shared controls and stopping rules

Both bundles require a signed freeze commit, manifest hash, role-separated
agent workspaces, no output sharing, source/access authorization references,
prohibited-data screening, exact receipts and a reviewer report. No historical
receipt hash may be reused for fresh evidence. No failed extraction or prior
rehearsal may be repaired. Missing authorized inputs stop preparation before
execution.

## Acceptance boundary

Successful fresh evidence may support an owner decision on M07/M10 within its
exact scope. It does not establish the approved four-route G2 C04/C07 pilot,
rights clearance, publication, release, lived-experience review or G2 passage.
The existing maturity rows remain L1 and the evidence register remains
`in_review` until fresh receipts and accountable adjudication exist.
