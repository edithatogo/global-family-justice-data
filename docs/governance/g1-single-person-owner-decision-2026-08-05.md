# G1 single-person owner decision — 2026-08-05

## Accountable decision

I, the repository owner and founder, act as host, sponsor, governance owner
and final accountable authority for the Global Family Justice Data project. I
retain decision rights for scope, methods, risk posture, publication and
release. No separate host, sponsor or institutional body is appointed.

This is a single-person repository. No human deputy, co-owner or substitute
approver is appointed. Analyst agents may provide operational continuity,
preparation, monitoring and advisory review, but they cannot approve gates,
sign legal or rights decisions, provide consent, create independent assurance
or authorize publication or release. Any unavailable-owner condition pauses
governed decisions.

The owner retains exclusive accountable authority for governance, methods
acceptance, residual-risk adjudication, rights-policy decisions, scope,
publication, custody, signing and release. Agent-panel outputs are advisory
inputs only and must include options, recommendation, rationale, contingencies,
evidence references and dissent.

The project remains aggregate-only and metadata-first. It will not intentionally
collect or publish personal, case-level, identifying, sensitive or participant
data. Unclear rights, privacy, security, disclosure or safeguarding states
remain quarantined or metadata-only. No result is promoted because it is
publicly discoverable.

I approve the bounded pilot universe recorded in
`data/seed/jurisdiction_register.csv`. Sources without exact-edition rights,
local verification or sufficient review remain descriptive, metadata-only or
quarantined. This approval does not claim complete global coverage.

Role-separated analyst-agent panels are the required assurance-preparation
mechanism for this single-person project. Their reports may identify
contradictions, risks and remediation options, but cannot be labelled
independent assurance or substitute for a legal, rights, consent,
human-participant or external authority record.

I accept the repository’s fail-closed residual-risk policy. Critical and high
risks remain blockers unless I record an explicit, risk-specific adjudication
with rationale, conditions, expiry and reopen trigger. This decision does not
waive rights, legal, security, privacy or release requirements.

I accept the documented single-person governance model, owner-held decision
rights, aggregate-only ethics/security boundary, agent-panel operating model,
bounded pilot scope and fail-closed controls. This is a conditional owner
decision. It does not by itself accept source rights, specialist assurance,
local verification, human review, real pilot evidence or release readiness. G1
remains blocked until its mandatory evidence and risk conditions are complete.

## Binding

This decision is bound to the repository commit containing this record and the
current `MANIFEST.sha256`. It changes the governance decision record to the
explicit single-person conditional model; it does not change G1 readiness.
