# Impact, harm and misuse monitoring plan

The project will monitor whether its outputs improve transparency while avoiding simplistic rankings, stigmatisation, unsafe disclosure or pressure to optimise speed at the expense of fairness and child safety. Monitoring covers citation and media use, stripped-context charts, jurisdiction complaints, correction patterns, user research, accessibility barriers, regional representation and evidence that measures are influencing harmful incentives.

Material misuse is triaged by the programme executive, methods lead, lived-experience advisers and security/data-governance owner. Responses may include contextual clarification, interface constraints, revised responsible-use guidance, correction, temporary withdrawal of a derived view, or a versioned methodological change. Raw released facts are never silently altered.
