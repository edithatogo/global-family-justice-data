## Why an enquiry remains required

The official Judicial Statistics Annual Report portal is identified, but its
family-case taxonomy, national court coverage, machine-readable availability,
revision semantics, and reuse permissions have not been reviewed to
completeness. The official website also states that its contents may not be
altered without Supreme Court consent.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — aggregate Japanese family-court statistics

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are documenting aggregate statistics published in Japan's
Judicial Statistics Annual Reports. Could you please confirm:

1. the official tables and case categories for domestic-relations adjudication
   and conciliation, personal-status litigation, child support, custody or
   visitation, adoption, guardianship, and child-return matters;
2. the courts, geographic coverage, reporting periods, missing values, and
   material definition or system changes in these series;
3. whether machine-readable tables, historical revisions, or an official data
   dictionary are available; and
4. the permitted attribution and reuse of statistical metadata, translated
   labels, unchanged tables, and transformed aggregate observations.

No identifiable, confidential, or case-level data is requested.

Regards,

[repository owner]
