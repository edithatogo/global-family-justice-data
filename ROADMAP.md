# Roadmap

## v0.1 — scaffold

- project plan, methods templates, schemas and seed sources;
- structural validation of registers.

## v0.2 — pilot source census

- 12 jurisdiction profiles;
- five years of priority source metadata;
- first bronze tables;
- public data-availability map.

## v0.3 — harmonised core indicators

- validated silver and gold observations;
- timeliness and backlog report;
- reproducible dashboard.

## v1.0 — first global census

- all in-scope jurisdictions assigned a coverage status;
- official source inventory and methods report;
- downloadable harmonised core dataset;
- archival release and DOI.

## v2.0 — outcomes programme

- user survey and legal-needs evidence catalogue;
- administrative-outcome crosswalk;
- selected lawful linked-data partnerships;
- annual international family-justice outcomes report.
