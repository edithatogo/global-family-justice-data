# Draft enquiry: South Africa Office of the Chief Justice

Status: **planned; not sent**

## Official route

- General access-to-information enquiries:
  `Enquiries [at] judiciary.org.za`.
- Formal PAIA route if the OCJ determines that a formal request is required:
  `Paia [at] judiciary.org.za`.

These routes are published in the
[OCJ PAIA manual](https://www.judiciary.org.za/index.php/documents/paia-manual).
The sender must re-check the current manual and use a formal PAIA request only
when necessary.

## Why an enquiry remains required

The annual-report portal is an official source, but the repository's coverage
assessment is partial and family jurisdiction varies across court levels and
related institutions. Public evidence does not establish a complete
family-matter series or definitive reuse terms.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — aggregate family-justice court statistics

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are mapping aggregate family-justice statistics published by
the South African Judiciary and Office of the Chief Justice. Could you please
confirm:

1. which courts and reports contain statistics for divorce, maintenance,
   domestic violence/protection orders, children's matters, and related family
   proceedings;
2. whether annual filings, finalisations, pending matters, clearance, and
   elapsed-time measures are available with consistent definitions;
3. which relevant series sit outside the OCJ, including the Department of
   Justice and Constitutional Development or Office of the Family Advocate;
   and
4. the attribution and reuse terms for published aggregate tables and derived
   observations, and whether machine-readable tables are available.

No personal, confidential, or case-level information is requested. Please
advise if this should instead be submitted through a formal PAIA process.

Regards,

[repository owner]
