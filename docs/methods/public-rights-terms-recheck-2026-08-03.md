  https://www.cnj.jus.br/pesquisas-judiciarias/justica-em-numeros/base-de-dados/

These findings narrow the rights classification but do not replace an
edition-specific accountable decision.

## Preservation rule

Public availability, a download link, or a reproduction statement for one
edition does not clear another edition or permit bulk redistribution. Each
retained file must have an edition identifier, lawful-access receipt, SHA-256,
terms URL and a rights decision. Ambiguity remains quarantined.
