# High-priority source rights review

Reviewed: 2026-08-01 Australia/Sydney (2026-07-31 UTC)

Reviewer: analyst-agent

This review records the publishers' public terms for the five high-priority
international sources still routed to rights review. It is not legal advice,
does not record an institutional permission, and does not authorise publication
or redistribution. Where the terms do not clearly cover database extracts, the
source remains fail-closed.

## Council of Europe / CEPEJ

Sources reviewed:

- `INT-CEPEJ-STAT`
- `INT-CEPEJ-2024`

Official terms:
https://www.coe.int/en/web/portal/copyright-licensing-permissions

The Council of Europe states that citation alone does not require permission
and that excerpts shorter than 500 words may be used with the specified credit.
It requires prior written permission to reproduce, republish, or translate
material from a publication or document. Non-commercial website material may
be reproduced for private or informational/educational use relating to the
Council's work if the source is identified and no charge is made; other uses,
including commercial use, require prior authorisation.

Disposition:

- `INT-CEPEJ-2024`: `restricted_or_unknown`; publication/table redistribution
  requires source-specific review and, unless an applicable exception is
  documented, prior permission.
- `INT-CEPEJ-STAT`: `unknown`; the general website terms do not establish a
  sufficiently specific licence for bulk database extraction or redistribution.
- Safe without further permission: metadata, source link, checksum, factual
  description, and compliant citation; an excerpt must stay within the stated
  exception and attribution conditions.

## National Center for State Courts

Sources reviewed:

- `INT-NCSC-COURTOOLS`
- `INT-NCSC-FAMILY`

Official terms:
https://www.ncsc.org/ncsc-library-collection-research-hub

NCSC's official library guidance says its resources may be copyright protected
and directs users to email the library to request copyright permission to use
NCSC materials. No open licence was identified on the reviewed CourTools or
Family Justice Performance Measures pages.

Disposition:

- Both sources remain `restricted_or_unknown`.
- Safe without further permission: metadata, source links, factual descriptions,
  and original GFJD definitions that do not reproduce protected NCSC expression.
- Reproduction or redistribution of NCSC tables, instruments, or substantial
  source content remains prohibited by project policy unless permission or a
  source-specific licence is recorded.

No permission request was sent. Any future request requires explicit owner
approval immediately before sending.

## Hague Conference on Private International Law

Source reviewed:

- `INT-HCCH-CA`

Official terms:

- https://www.hcch.net/en/disclaimer/
- https://www.hcch.net/en/publications-and-studies/publications2/general-terms-conditions-sale/general-terms-conditions-sale-digital-publications

The HCCH website notice authorises reproduction of website information for
non-commercial purposes with acknowledgement. The publications guidance is
narrower: personal copies and attributed excerpts have specified allowances,
commercial reproduction requires written consent, and purchased digital
publications prohibit reproduction, distribution, sharing, or resale without
prior written consent.

Disposition:

- `INT-HCCH-CA` remains `restricted_or_unknown` because the source combines
  portal information, statistics, and publications with different terms.
- Metadata, links, and attributed non-commercial use of website information can
  be routed separately from publication or dataset bytes.
- Publication files, database extracts, and redistributable derived tables stay
  blocked pending source-edition classification and, where required, permission.

## Remaining decision boundary

These reviews replace a wholly unknown position with documented restrictions
and permitted metadata/linking routes, but they do not clear any source for
general redistribution. Before acquisition or release, each selected edition
still needs a rights record bound to its exact URL, retrieval date, checksum,
content class, intended use, and attribution. Permission-dependent use remains
an external gate.
