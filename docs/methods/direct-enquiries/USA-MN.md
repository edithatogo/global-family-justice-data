# Draft enquiry: Minnesota Judicial Branch

Status: **planned; not sent**

## Official route

- Statewide statistics: the official
  [Court Statistics and Reports](https://www.mncourts.gov/Help-Topics/Data-Requests/)
  page provides the “Request Data” route to the State Court Administrator.
- Hennepin/Fourth District questions: use the official
  [Fourth Judicial District Public Data Request](https://www.mncourts.gov/Find-Courts/Fourth-Judicial-District/Public-Data-Request.aspx)
  form.

The statewide page says requests receive a status response within two business
days and may involve a fee. The sender must record the form confirmation or
official ticket identifier.

## Why an enquiry remains required

Public dashboards and reports have been found, but the repository's assessment
is partial and does not establish statewide family-case coverage, consistent
matter definitions, or a reusable historical series. The public site also
distinguishes dashboards, bulk extracts, and bespoke data requests.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD data enquiry — Minnesota aggregate family-court statistics

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are documenting aggregate Minnesota family-court statistics.
Could you please confirm:

1. the statewide case categories corresponding to divorce/dissolution,
   custody or parenting, maintenance/support, adoption, child protection, and
   protection-order matters;
2. available annual counts for filings, dispositions, pending cases, and
   processing time, including definition or system changes;
3. whether the public dashboards cover all ten districts and whether
   Hennepin-specific reports use the same definitions; and
4. the permitted reuse and attribution terms for downloaded CSV/XLSX/PDF
   aggregates and whether a data-use agreement is required for the intended
   public derived observations.

No identifiable or case-level data is requested.

Regards,

[repository owner]
