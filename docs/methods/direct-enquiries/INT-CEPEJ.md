## Why an enquiry remains required

The repository has located CEPEJ-STAT and an official report fallback, but the
fallback is explicitly not equivalent to the raw database. The coverage
assessment is partial, family-justice detail varies, and rights remain unknown.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD enquiry — CEPEJ-STAT family-justice scope and permitted use

Hello CEPEJ Secretariat,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are assessing CEPEJ-STAT as a source of aggregate court-system
statistics and would be grateful if you could confirm:

1. which CEPEJ-STAT variables, case categories, and country comments cover
   family, matrimonial, parental-responsibility, maintenance, or related cases;
2. whether a versioned bulk export or documented query route is available and
   how revisions or suppressed values should be interpreted;
3. whether we may publish source metadata, query parameters, checksums, and
   derived aggregate observations while linking to CEPEJ-STAT; and
4. the required attribution and whether permission is needed to reproduce
   selected tables or redistribute an unchanged export.

We will not publish source files or observations unless the applicable terms
permit it. Please identify any conditions, licence, or preferred permissions
route.

Regards,

[repository owner]
