# G2 blind-holdout intake stop — owner decision packet — 2026-08-15

Status: awaiting sole-owner decision

The approved metadata-only intake stopped because the frozen 24-primary and

## Option A — prepare a successor two-stage structural preflight

Recommended. Preserve the stopped intake, then prepare a new prospective
design that freezes an oversampled metadata frame and deterministic order
before any bytes are acquired. A later, separate owner decision would authorize
an acquisition custodian and isolated eligibility reviewers to verify exact
edition identity and structural class without target extraction or
transcription. Fresh extraction agents would remain blind to all preflight
work. The first qualifying 24 primaries and six reserves would be selected in
the frozen order.

Trade-off: this resolves the circular requirement that raster structure be
known before a PDF may be opened, but changes “unseen” from no project-wide
inspection to no exposure by either extraction path. That limitation must be
explicit in every claim.

Contingency: if a prospectively oversized frame still cannot yield the exact
24+6 scope, stop without substitution, waiver or post-inspection redesign and
return to the owner for exclusion or termination.

## Option B — one capped metadata-only sweep

Search additional official HTML catalogues and public rights pages without
accessing source files. Preserve the current design unchanged.

Trade-off: strongest continuity with the current definition of unseen, but the
first 58 candidates yielded only one metadata-supported raster candidate, 43
uncertain rights screens and 41 missing exact source URLs. Another pass is
unlikely to satisfy the exact scope and may create further search-snippet
exposures.

Contingency: cap the pass in advance. If it still cannot assemble the scope,
choose Option A, C or D; do not continue searching indefinitely.

## Option C — replace the content-dependent stratum

Prospectively redesign the study around strata that official metadata can
verify, such as explicitly labelled infographics or accessible/text-native
PDFs.

Trade-off: simplest metadata-only selection and lower preflight exposure, but
it removes or narrows the intended raster/layout stress test and weakens the
generalisation claim.

Contingency: retain raster/dashboard handling as a later separate challenge
study rather than treating the redesigned holdout as evidence for it.

## Option D — defer or terminate

Preserve the valid stop and perform no further candidate work. G2 remains
blocked.

## Panel recommendation and rationale

Choose Option A, but authorize preparation of the successor design only. Do not
authorize source-byte acquisition yet. Both role-separated panels concluded
that another metadata-only pass has low probability of resolving the structural
and exact-edition circularity. A prospectively frozen two-stage protocol can
preserve deterministic selection, role isolation, thresholds, no-waiver rules,
quarantine and publication boundaries while making the structural eligibility
test possible.
