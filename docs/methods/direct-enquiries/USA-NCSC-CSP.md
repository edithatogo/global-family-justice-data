## Why an enquiry remains required

The project provides a national comparative caseload framework, but the
repository's assessment remains partial. State participation, family-specific
scope, comparability of domestic-relations and juvenile categories, historical
coverage, revisions, and public reuse terms remain unresolved.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — NCSC Court Statistics Project family-case data

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are documenting aggregate U.S. state-court family-justice
statistics using the Court Statistics Project. Could you please confirm:

1. which CSP case types cover divorce/dissolution, custody or parenting,
   support, adoption, child protection, domestic violence, and related family
   or juvenile matters;
2. which annual measures are public for incoming, outgoing, pending, and aged
   cases, and where reporting-guide or taxonomy changes are documented;
3. which states, court tiers, years, and case types are incomplete or not
   publishable, and how revisions or missing submissions are represented; and
4. the permitted reuse and attribution terms for dashboard downloads,
   reporting-guide material, and public aggregate derived observations.

No identifiable or case-level data is requested, and we are not asking CSP to
disclose state submissions that are not already public. A response may point
to existing public documentation instead of creating a bespoke dataset.

Regards,

[repository owner]
