# Data governance and ethics

## Public repository boundary

The public repository is for source metadata, aggregate observations, code and documentation. It must not contain names, case numbers linked to individuals, addresses, dates of birth, protected judgments, sealed records or small cells that create a credible re-identification risk.

## Children and vulnerable parties

Family-justice data can reveal safety, care, violence, migration, disability and health circumstances. The project should use a child-rights and trauma-informed governance model, not merely legal minimum compliance.

## Linked data

Any person-level linkage programme requires a separate secure environment, lawful basis, ethics and data-custodian approvals, disclosure controls and project-specific governance. Only safe aggregate outputs and study metadata belong in this repository.

## Responsible comparison

- avoid causal claims from descriptive administrative statistics;
- publish uncertainty and missingness;
- explain legal and procedural context;
- do not treat high filing rates automatically as poor performance or high clearance automatically as good outcomes;
- avoid composite rankings until validity and consequences have been independently assessed.
