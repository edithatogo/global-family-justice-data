# G2 prospective semantic-contract grouped owner decision — 2026-08-27

## Outcome prepared for decision

The role-separated agent panel recommends a deliberately minimal prospective
contract. Explicit source concepts map to `source_defined`; absent or
conflicting concepts map to `unknown`. Components remain empty until their
labels, locators and provenance can be represented. Every calibration row is
hard-quarantined. The existing 100% critical and 99% populated-field thresholds
remain unchanged.

The contract, schema, executable validator and adversarial tests are prepared.
They do not authorize execution. The draft was informed by prior difference
categories, so the same failed cohort cannot be reused and semantic leakage
cannot be claimed to have been independently excluded.

## Options

### Option A — minimal deterministic contract (recommended)

Approve the prepared `source_defined`/`unknown` ontology, ordered evidence and
conflict rules, truthful nullable locator/cohort changes, empty-only components
and universal hard quarantine for calibration.

Trade-off: this maximizes reproducibility and minimizes unsupported inference,
but deliberately yields little controlled semantic detail. A future calibration
must use different materials selected after a separate signed freeze.

Contingency: if even this minimal contract remains nondeterministic, stop coded
semantic calibration and use source-native extraction only for ontology
discovery.

### Option B — develop a richer ontology first

Split domains, matters, indicators, cohorts, populations and components into a
larger hierarchy before calibration.

Trade-off: potentially more useful data, but much greater ambiguity, owner
burden and risk of encoding observed examples. This is not recommended until a
separate source-independent ontology exercise exists.

### Option C — source-native-only calibration

Retain source labels and quotations and avoid substantive controlled coding.

Trade-off: strongest fidelity and safest fallback, but it cannot establish
meaningful controlled-semantic concordance and should be labelled ontology
discovery rather than G2 evidence.

### Option D — relax thresholds or adjudicate after extraction

Rejected. It would weaken the precommitted claim and turn exact concordance
into negotiated concordance.
