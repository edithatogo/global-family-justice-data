# Next evidence-intake options — 2026-08-03

## Options

| Option | Approach | Benefit | Risk/contingency |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Full five-candidate real pilot immediately | Broadest evidence base | High access, rights and review burden; quarantine incomplete candidates |
| B | One evidence-complete candidate first | Fastest route to a defensible pilot | Limited generalisability; keep other candidates queued |
| C | Metadata-only intake for all candidates | Low rights and access risk | Cannot satisfy G2 real-pilot or G3 coverage criteria |
| D | Hybrid: one evidence-complete candidate plus dual-route capture for the rest | Best balance of progress, redundancy and fail-closed safety | Requires strict scope separation and separate receipts |

## Recommendation

Use **Option D**. Select the first candidate only after a complete edition,
rights class, extraction recipe and reviewer availability are confirmed. Run
dual-route capture for the remaining candidates, retaining metadata-only or
quarantined status where access or rights are unresolved.

## Contingencies

- No candidate reaches evidence-complete status → remain metadata-only and
  continue public-source preparation.
- Rights unclear → retain citation/metadata only; do not upload bytes.
- Reviewer unavailable → keep the item in review and do not promote.
- Primary route blocked → use the recorded fallback or preserve an access-failure
  receipt; do not bypass authentication/CAPTCHA.
- Owner decision fields incomplete → retain G1 conditional and record the exact
  missing fields.

This recommendation is advisory preparation. It does not create authority,
rights, local verification, methods assurance or a gate decision.
