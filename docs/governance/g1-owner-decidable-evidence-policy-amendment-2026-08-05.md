# G1 owner-decidable evidence policy amendment — 2026-08-05

The repository owner approves a scoped amendment for this single-person
repository.

For owner-decidable G1 criteria—host/sponsor role, owner-held decision rights,
single-person operating model, no-deputy exception, bounded pilot scope,
aggregate-only boundary, repository operating policy and owner-controlled
residual-risk process—an owner-signed, digest-bound decision record may satisfy
accountable acceptance without a second human reviewer.

Analyst-agent panels remain advisory and must provide options, recommendation,
rationale, contingencies, evidence references, conflicts, abstentions and
dissent. They cannot be labelled independent assurance.

This amendment does not waive rights, legal, privacy, security, safeguarding,
consent, local verification, accessibility, specialist methods assurance, real
pilot evidence, custody, staffing, funding or release-authority records. Those
criteria remain separately required and fail-closed.

The allowlist is deliberately machine-readable in `config/project.toml` and is
limited to `E-GOV-CHARTER`, `E-RACI`, `E-TRACK-CHARTERS` and
`E-PILOT-UNIVERSE`. Any expansion requires a new owner decision.
