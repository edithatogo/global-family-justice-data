# G2 historical-edition proposal: role-separated advisory panel

Date: 2026-08-30. Scope: repository-only methods, exposure/operations and
governance advice. No publisher, search-provider or source-content access.
These are analyst-agent opinions, not independent assurance or owner acceptance.

## Methods — historical_methods_advice

Recommend a new prospective historical-edition eligibility design, not a claim
that an entirely new discovery method or executable cohort already exists.
The 2026-08-20 frame returned 0/96: every one of its 20 source-register URLs was
already in the 672-URL exposure set. Dropping a date cutoff does not undo that.

Freeze different material selection before discovery; exclude all pre-existing
exposed identities and failed cohorts. Distinguish new registrar observations
inside the new campaign from exposure predating its freeze. New observations
become permanent exposure for every later campaign. No content-driven selection
of easy examples. Preserve 100% critical and at least 99% populated concordance.

Dissent: this is a prospective eligibility redesign, not automatically a
materially distinct method. Bounded reproducibility does not establish model-
training unseenness, broad generalisation, rights or G2 acceptance.

## Exposure and operations — historical_exposure_advice

Prefer a newly frozen historical-window request to the existing GOV.UK official
statistics index template over broad national sitemaps. The repository already
records the host, path, family-justice taxonomy and complete single-page controls
in `config/g2_official_publication_feed_monitor.json`. Its future-only URL is
not authority for a historical request. No endpoint was visited during this review.

Known child-sitemap locators are control roots, not unseen candidate editions.
The failed broad-root registration records 35 GOV.UK child URLs; lack of a
traversal receipt for a child is not proof that its descendants are unexposed.
Public timestamps may be updates rather than first-edition publication dates.

The old JSON exposure builder is not a complete importer for newer JSONL monitor
ledgers. Include current monitor results, aliases, partial failures and failed
cohorts in a newly verified cumulative bundle. A few configured JSON/JSONL paths
are not proof of complete historical coverage. Missing coverage stops execution.

## Governance — historical_governance_advice

Repository preparation needs no new approval. Historical execution changes the
explicit future-publication rule and cannot inherit the existing authorization.
Prepare one grouped staged authorization only after endpoints, exposure,
selection, source interlocks, roles, schemas, budgets and stopping rules are
fully bound. Do not solicit authorization against missing hashes or placeholders.

A passing run returns for grouped owner adjudication. No source-rights clearance,
publication, release, maturity promotion or G2 acceptance follows from this plan.

## Grouped recommendation, trade-offs and contingency

1. Recommend preparing the bounded official-index historical route. It could
   remove the calendar wait, but might return no eligible material or exceed the
   cap; the proposed UK-only metadata stage cannot silently redefine the pilot.
2. Retain the existing future-edition monitors as redundancy. Their already
   exposed candidates cannot be retrospectively recycled into this route.
3. Reject reuse of failed outputs or reclassification of exposed editions as
   unseen. It would change the evidence claim rather than resolve the blocker.

If complete non-overlap, authoritative identity, scope alignment or safe source
access cannot be established, stop. Do not adapt the window, repair the failed
result or promote a subset. Return a consolidated decision only for a genuinely
different prospective option.
