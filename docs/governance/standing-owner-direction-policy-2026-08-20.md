# Standing owner direction and proportional-decision policy — 2026-08-20

## Authority and purpose

The repository owner and sole accountable decision-maker directs the project to
minimise approval spam. This policy turns the owner's ongoing instruction to
continue safely into standing direction for routine repository work. It
supersedes only the *procedural expectation* of repeated owner packets for
routine work; it does not rewrite historical evidence or lower any gate.

## Standing direction

Without a further owner decision, agents may perform and record:

- repository-owned planning, implementation, tests, validation, documentation
  and generated-artifact refreshes;
- evidence indexing, provenance checks, risk/control updates and transparent
  fail-closed outcomes;
- role-separated advisory panel work and remediation of its repository-owned
  findings;
- private, no-external-access preparation and synthetic rehearsals within the
  established aggregate-only boundary;
- signed commits, pull requests, hosted-check observation, merge of passing
  repository changes, and routine cleanup of merged branches and worktrees.

An updated digest, a new test result, an advisory report, a retry permitted by
an existing stop rule, or a private fail-closed result does not by itself
create a new owner-approval requirement. Record it in the appropriate evidence
or implementation record and continue.

## Decisions that remain explicit

Ask the owner once, in a concise grouped form, only before:

1. a new external request, outbound contact, source-content/file access or
   network activity not already authorized by an existing bounded decision;
2. accepting third-party terms, using paid services, credentials or expenditure;
3. public publication, deployment, signing, release, destructive data removal
   or an irreversible external state change;
4. materially expanding scope, changing the aggregate-only/privacy boundary,
   weakening a safety control, or changing a risk policy; or
5. accepting a programme gate, waiving a mandatory criterion, clearing rights,
   or adjudicating a residual critical/high risk.

Existing bounded external authorizations remain valid only for their recorded
scope and stop rules. If such a rule stops, agents may preserve and analyse the
result but must not silently broaden the external activity.

## Decision hygiene

- Do not produce a decision packet when there is no meaningful owner choice.
- When a decision is necessary, group related choices and lead with the
  recommendation, trade-off and contingency in a short handoff.
- Agent-panel findings are advisory inputs, not additional approval gates.
- Keep actual factual evidence, rights conditions and gate status explicit;
  proportional procedure does not make a pending gate pass.

## Current effect

This policy applies to all active Conductor tracks. It authorizes the normal
repository lifecycle through merge, but it does not authorize G2 external
access, source acquisition, publication, release or gate passage. Those remain
subject to their existing scope controls or a single future grouped owner
decision when a genuine external action is ready.
