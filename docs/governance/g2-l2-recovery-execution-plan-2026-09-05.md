# G2 L2 M07/M10 recovery and fresh-evidence execution plan

This is a repository-owned execution plan and advisory disposition. It does
not reconstruct historical receipts, establish M07 or M10 maturity, accept
G2, or authorize external access, publication or release.

## Current blocker

The availability audit verified the baseline evidence register and located the
terminal M06 receipt, but did not find the underlying M07 product rehearsal or
M10 language-review receipts in the bounded repository scan. The narrative
references and historical hashes are retained as lineage only. They must not
be used to recreate, backfill or re-date either receipt.

## Role-separated advisory options

| Option | Action | Trade-offs and limitations | Contingency |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — recover | Locate the original receipt and its bound artifacts in an owner-controlled retained backup; verify the exact historical digest, input bindings and artifact availability. | Preserves historical provenance and is the lowest-cost route, but a receipt without its inputs may still be non-reproducible. | If either digest or required inputs fail verification, classify that lineage unrecoverable and do not promote it. |
| B — fresh bounded evidence (recommended) | Freeze a new M07 product/accessibility bundle and a separate M10 source-language/localisation bundle, with new evidence IDs, dates and hashes; execute only after the owner authorizes the exact inputs and access. | Produces current, honest evidence but cannot prove the historical event and cannot substitute for human lived-experience, native/local-human, legal or independent assurance. | If an input, source-language edition, local context record or resource commitment is unavailable, stop before execution and return the missing-input list. |
| C — retain status quo | Keep both dimensions in review and continue repository-owned preparation only. | No access risk, but does not unblock L2 or downstream gates. | Revisit when owner-controlled inputs become available. |

Recommendation: attempt Option A only against an explicitly identified
owner-controlled backup. In parallel, prepare Option B as the executable
contingency; use Option C whenever either route lacks its required input. The
panel recommendation is not independent assurance and cannot create a human,
local, native-speaker, accessibility-participant, legal or specialist record.

## Fresh M07 bundle (repository-owned preparation)

Before execution, bind a signed freeze commit and manifest containing the exact
product build, locked toolchain, input hashes, two independently produced
manifests, per-file SHA-256 and byte length, media types, deterministic order,
retrieval procedure and retention boundary. Run two fresh isolated builds,
compare manifests exactly, then perform a separate clean retrieval check.
Record only aggregate metadata and receipts in Git. Accessibility automation
is evidence of automated checks only; it is not lived-experience or manual
assistive-technology review.

Required stop conditions: any digest, ordering, schema, prohibited-data,
retrieval or manifest mismatch; missing owner authorization; unavailable
inputs; or an attempt to reuse a historical output.

## Fresh M10 bundle (repository-owned preparation)

Before execution, bind exact source-language editions or already authorized
metadata, locators, source-language observations, publisher/registry
triangulation records, scope distinctions, the owner resource commitment and
its validity period. Record translation uncertainty, missing-language limits,
local-review absence, dissent and unresolved registry identity explicitly.

Agent-panel advice may identify contradictions and options, but cannot be
called native-speaker, local-human, institutional, independent or specialist
assurance. Any unresolved source identity, translation, scope or resource
period discrepancy keeps M10 in review.

## Decision and evidence boundary

The owner must separately decide whether to authorize the exact recovery
locator or fresh bundle inputs, any source/network access, and the resulting
bounded evidence acceptance. Until then:

- `E-G2-L2-AVAILABILITY-20260905` remains `in_review`;
- the M07/M10 maturity rows remain at their current level;
- no historical receipt is reconstructed or assigned its old hash;
- no G2 C04/C07, publication, release or downstream gate is promoted.

If fresh execution is authorized and passes its frozen controls, the resulting
receipt must return for owner adjudication with its manifest hash, process
receipt, reviewer/advisory report, limitations and exact acceptance scope.
