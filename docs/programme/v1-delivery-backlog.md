## Prioritisation rule

At every planning cycle:

1. unblock the critical path and release criteria;
2. remove P0/P1 risk;
3. finish partially completed evidence-bearing work;
4. reduce manual/key-person dependency;
5. expand coverage and gold data;
6. improve presentation and optional analytics.

A new visible feature should not outrank missing provenance, review, rights or recovery work.
