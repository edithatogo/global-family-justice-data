# Non-substitutable evidence sourcing plan — 2026-08-03

This plan sources evidence that repository code, fixtures and agent-panel advice
cannot create. It uses independent routes for triangulation, but does not
pretend that two weak sources equal accountable authority.

## Route design

Each item has a primary route, a triangulation route, a fallback and an owner
decision point. Every receipt is bound to date, identity/role, scope, exact
artifact and SHA-256 where bytes exist. Outbound contact remains forbidden
unless the owner authorises the exact recipient list immediately before send.

## Sequencing

1. Close the owner packet and governance records (G1).
2. Freeze the pilot scope and edition list.
3. Acquire or verify exact source editions and rights dispositions.
4. Run panel methods, rights, security and accessibility passes.
5. Obtain local/multilingual verification and complete enquiry closures.
6. Execute the real pilot, re-extraction and row-level reconciliation.
7. Obtain operations, custody, signing, safeguarding, staffing and funding records.
8. Rebuild the candidate, bind all receipts and request owner adjudication.

## Triangulation rules

- A second route corroborates provenance or interpretation; it does not create a
  missing authority.
- Conflicting routes produce a quarantine disposition and an owner decision.
- A no-response is evidence of non-response, never evidence of permission or
  coverage.
- Panel consensus is advisory and cannot satisfy a field explicitly requiring
  human consent, legal permission, specialist sign-off or signed custody.

## Gate contingencies

If any required item remains unavailable, retain the current gate block, narrow
claims to evidence-complete subsets, keep restricted material metadata-only,
and do not publish, sign, host or promote the release candidate.
