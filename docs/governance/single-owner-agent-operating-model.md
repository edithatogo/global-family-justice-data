# Single-owner and agent operating model

**Effective date:** 2026-08-01  
**Accountable owner:** repository owner  
**Execution model:** autonomous analyst agents and automated controls

This repository is operated by one accountable owner. There are no assumed
human deputies or institutional sponsors. Agent roles are execution and review
assignments, not delegated legal or governance authority.

## Decision rights

- The repository owner is the final authority for scope, formal gate decisions,
  exceptions, publication, takedown, and any outbound enquiry.
- Analyst agents may search, acquire, checksum, map, validate, draft, test, and
  prepare decision packets.
- An agent may not convert draft evidence into accepted gate evidence, promote
  observations to gold, grant source redistribution rights, or send an email
  without an explicit owner decision recorded in the repository.
- Agent validation is independent technical review only; it does not substitute
  for owner adjudication or external assurance.

## Operating consequence

The owner-approved pilot and global universe remain in scope, but readiness is
fail-closed. A jurisdiction advances only when its evidence, rights treatment,
review ledger, and owner adjudication satisfy the declared contracts.

This model is the controlling contingency for unavailable human deputies: work
continues autonomously, while authority-dependent actions remain blocked and
are surfaced as decisions rather than inferred approvals.
