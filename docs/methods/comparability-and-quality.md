# Comparability and quality

## Source quality grade

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Official, machine-readable, documented and stable. |
| B | Official table or publication with usable definitions. |
| C | Official source requiring interpretation or manual extraction. |
| D | Credible research/non-government source. |
| E | Secondary, unverified or inadequately documented. |

## Comparability tier

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Direct comparison is reasonable. Matter type, clock, cohort, statistic and denominator align. |
| 2 | Comparison is possible with a transparent restriction or transformation. |
| 3 | Descriptive juxtaposition only. Material definitional differences remain. |
| 4 | Not comparable. Retain for local analysis and source mapping. |

## Minimum checks for a duration measure

- What event starts the clock?
- What event stops the clock?
- Are inactive, stayed or administratively closed periods excluded?
- Is the unit a case, application, child, family or order?
- Is the cohort completed in the period or filed in the period?
- Is the statistic mean, median, percentile or threshold proportion?
- Are urgent, consent, uncontested or transferred matters included?
- Is the measure national, court-level or registry-level?

## Release rule

A value may enter the gold layer only when its source, definition, calculation, mapping, quality grade and comparability tier are complete and independently reviewed.
