# Draft enquiry: Swedish National Courts Administration

Status: **planned; not sent**

## Official route

- Send the enquiry to the statistics support address published on the official
  [court-statistics page](https://www.domstol.se/domstolsverket/om-sveriges-domstolar/statistik-styrning-och-utveckling/statistik/officiell-domstolsstatistik/):
  `statistik@dom.se`.
- If delivery fails, use the administration's official
  [contact route](https://www.domstol.se/domstolsverket/oppettider-och-kontakt/)
  at `domstolsverket@dom.se` and ask that it be routed to statistics support.

The statistics page identifies Domstolsverket as the authority responsible for
official court statistics and says older quality declarations and unpublished
reports may be requested from statistics support. Record the sent message,
delivery evidence, and any official case or registration number.

## Why an enquiry remains required

The official statistics page and statistical database are public, but the
repository's assessment remains partial. It does not yet establish which
family-law matter types are separately reportable, whether definitions and
counting rules are stable over time, or the permitted attribution and reuse of
downloaded tables in public derived observations.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — Swedish aggregate family-court statistics

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are documenting aggregate Swedish court statistics using the
official court-statistics page and database. Could you please confirm:

1. which published case categories cover divorce, custody, residence/contact,
   maintenance, adoption, child-protection, and protection-order matters;
2. which annual measures are available for incoming, determined, and pending
   cases and processing time, including definition or system changes;
3. whether the database covers all relevant courts and whether omitted or
   suppressed categories are documented; and
4. the permitted reuse and attribution terms for downloaded tables and
   quality declarations in public derived observations.

No identifiable, confidential, or case-level information is requested. A
response may refer us to existing public documentation instead of creating new
data.

Regards,

[repository owner]

## Evidence and closure

Retain the sent message, delivery evidence, any registration number, response,
and reviewer decision in the controlled evidence store. Allow 15 calendar days
for an initial response, send one follow-up through the same official route,
then allow a further 15 calendar days. Only after those **15 + 15 days** may a
reviewer record `closed_no_response`. A delivery failure requires correction
or an alternative official route and is not a no-response closure.
