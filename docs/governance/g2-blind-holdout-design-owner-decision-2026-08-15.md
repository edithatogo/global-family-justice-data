## Owner decision

I approve Option A in the G2 blind-holdout design owner decision packet dated
2026-08-15. I approve the design frozen at signed commit
`42b89486b7d71a60ed01eb7e7b1d862e6a736820`: 24 primary unseen official
aggregate PDF editions, one row per edition, six editions in each frozen
stratum, at least 12 jurisdictions, no more than two editions per jurisdiction,
one edition per source series, and six stratum-matched pre-ranked reserves.

I retain 100% critical and at least 99% overall populated-field concordance,
exact comparison, no fuzzy matching or critical waiver, the complete exposure
denylist, fresh artifact-isolated agent roles, and every frozen stopping rule.
Any inability to assemble the scope stops and returns to me; the design must
not silently shrink or inspect content to improve eligibility.

I authorize metadata-only public candidate discovery, deterministic ranking,
exposure-ledger construction, non-overlap verification and public terms,
rights, privacy and security pre-screening. Candidate work must not download,
open, render or transcribe source content.

This decision does not authorize source-byte acquisition, source-content
inspection, extraction, outbound contact, publication, release, rights
acceptance or G2 passage. Before execution I must receive and separately
approve the exact digest-bound primary/reserve manifest, complete exposure and
selection receipts, edition-specific screens, frozen contracts and comparator,
role bundles and access controls, resource estimate, execution packet and
signed freeze commit.
