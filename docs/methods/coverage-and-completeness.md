# Coverage and completeness standard

## 1. Purpose

“Internationally complete” must describe the search and classification process, not pretend that every country publishes every measure. This standard defines how the project can make a global coverage claim without manufacturing data or hiding silence.

## 2. Jurisdiction universe

The methods group approves and versions the universe before the global census. It includes:

- sovereign jurisdictions;
- dependent/associated jurisdictions under an explicit rule;
- separate national legal systems where relevant;
- states, provinces, territories, cantons or other subnational systems with material responsibility for family justice;
- selected international/transnational reporting systems represented as a separate level.

The register distinguishes a parent political entity from the actual justice system responsible for a matter.

## 3. Coverage unit

The coverage unit is a jurisdiction-system-by-matter-domain search record. A jurisdiction may be verified complete for divorce reporting but still have unresolved child-protection or protection-order searches.

The public headline status is the most cautious current aggregate status; detailed domain statuses remain available.

## 4. Status definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `not_started` | No documented search has begun |
| `search_in_progress` | Search is active or incomplete |
| `official_source_found` | At least one in-scope official source located; completeness review not necessarily finished |
| `non_government_source_only` | Relevant evidence located but no in-scope official source after documented search |
| `no_public_source_found` | No relevant public source located after full protocol and second review |
| `source_inaccessible` | Source is known but cannot be accessed, reproduced or lawfully used |
| `direct_contact_pending` | Public search is complete and clarification/data request is awaiting response |
| `verified_complete` | Search protocol, institutional map and second review complete for the stated cycle |

`verified_complete` does not mean that all indicators are available. It means the search and source inventory are complete to the approved standard for that cycle.

## 5. Search completeness evidence

A verified record includes:

- institutional map;
- search date range and reviewer;
- languages and search terms;
- official domains and publication families checked;
- candidate sources and exclusion reasons;
- direct enquiries where required;
- unresolved access/translation questions;
- second reviewer and review date;
- next scheduled review date.

## 6. Negative findings

A negative finding is publishable only when:

- the responsible institutions were identified;
- local-language and English searches were attempted as appropriate;
- major official publication channels were checked;
- related matter terminology was used rather than only “family court”;
- likely non-digital/annual-report routes were considered;
- a second reviewer repeated or challenged the search;
- the result is dated and can expire.

Negative findings should be phrased as “no public source located under the protocol as of [date]”, not “the jurisdiction publishes no data” unless an authoritative institution confirms that fact.

## 7. Completeness metrics

The programme reports:

- percentage of jurisdiction universe with any current status;
- percentage with completed institutional maps;
- percentage verified complete for the cycle;
- percentage of negative findings second-reviewed;
- distribution by region, legal system, income/administrative capacity and language coverage;
- domains searched and sources found per jurisdiction;
- source machine-readability and update frequency;
- age of last review;
- unresolved access and translation gaps.

A global average must not conceal regions with materially weaker verification.

## 8. Review cycle

- High-change or high-priority jurisdictions: at least annual full review.
- Stable source systems: annual health check and full review at least every two years.
- Broken source, institutional reform or major legal change: immediate status review.
- Every public status exposes the as-of date.

## 9. v1.0 threshold

v1.0 requires:

- 100% of the approved universe has exactly one current headline status;
- every negative/inaccessible status has second-review evidence;
- known domain-level gaps are published;
- all regions have accountable review coverage;
- no blank is interpreted as zero or absence;
- the universe, status vocabulary and review-cycle snapshot are included in the immutable release.
