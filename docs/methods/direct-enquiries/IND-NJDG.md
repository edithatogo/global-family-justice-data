## Why an enquiry remains required

NJDG is an official public source, but the repository's assessment remains
partial. Family-law taxonomy, High Court and subordinate-court coverage,
historical definition changes, missing-data semantics, export availability,
and permitted reuse and attribution remain unresolved.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — NJDG aggregate family-justice statistics

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. Please route this enquiry to the NJDG/e-Courts team responsible for
the public statistical data. Could that team confirm:

1. which NJDG case types or subject classifications cover divorce,
   matrimonial disputes, custody/guardianship, maintenance, adoption, child
   protection, and domestic-violence matters;
2. which annual measures and exports are available for institutions, disposals,
   pending cases, and case age or duration, including taxonomy or system
   changes;
3. whether the public grid covers every relevant High Court and district or
   subordinate court, and how missing, delayed, or incomplete court returns are
   represented; and
4. the permitted reuse and attribution terms for downloaded public aggregates
   and derived observations.

No names, case numbers, party details, orders, or other identifiable or
case-level information are requested. A response may refer us to existing
public documentation rather than prepare a bespoke extract.

Regards,

[repository owner]
