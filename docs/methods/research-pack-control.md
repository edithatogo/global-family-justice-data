# Research-pack control

The jurisdiction research pack is a non-evidentiary handoff artifact. It
combines registered institutions, candidate sources, and language/domain search
guidance for an analyst. It does not record that a source was found, that a
coverage state is true, or that a jurisdiction is complete. Search results must
be entered separately in the controlled census logs and reviewed before they
affect readiness.

Any generated pack remains `non_evidentiary_handoff` and carries limitations
that prohibit promotion to gate evidence.
