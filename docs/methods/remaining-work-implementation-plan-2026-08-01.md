# Remaining work implementation plan

This plan is the execution register for the unresolved programme issues. It
distinguishes repository preparation from evidence or authority that must come
from outside the repository.

| Sequence | Issue | Repository implementation | External input/approval | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Governance and gate authority | Maintain digest-bound decision packets and gate status | Owner/deputy identities, signed acceptance | Keep gate in review |
| 2 | Methods and pilot evidence | Freeze candidate methods packet; run subagent panel and conflict matrix | Independent methods authority and real pilot evidence | Quarantine unresolved questions |
| 3 | Coverage and enquiries | Review search logs, build maps/assessments and preserve response receipts | Local verification and approved follow-up scope | Unresolved/inaccessible; dated no-response closure |
| 4 | Source rights | Capture official terms, hashes and proposed dispositions | Rights/legal decision per source | Metadata-only or exclude |
| 5 | Quality and re-extraction | Run deterministic validation, quarantine and reproducibility checks | Independent re-extraction/adjudication | Descriptive-only/quarantine |
| 6 | Product assurance | Build verified candidate products with limitations and correction links | Human accessibility, localisation, usability and harms review | Candidate-only; no publication |
| 7 | Security and provenance | Run security, lock, manifest, backup and release controls | Independent legal/privacy/security/supply-chain assurance and signing authority | Unsigned local candidate |
| 8 | Operations and custody | Rehearse release, restore and correction workflows | Host, monitoring, incident channel, two-location custody and named owners | No deployment/archive |
| 9 | Participation and sustainability | Maintain consent/safeguarding templates and costed operating plan | Regional/lived-experience participation, staffing, deputies and funding | Synthetic rehearsal; evidence remains missing |
| 10 | Final release and archive | Rebuild exact digest-bound candidate and evidence index | Final accountable release approval | Keep all tracks open and fail-closed |

## Execution controls

- Public-source research records URL, title, language, date, access result,
  coverage period, rights status and digest where bytes are acquired.
- Every subagent report is role-scoped, packet-digest-bound and advisory; panel
  consensus cannot provide legal, scientific, human-consent or publication
  authority.
- No outbound message is sent without an exact recipient list, approved
  template/scope and send authorization.
- Unknown, inaccessible, unverified or disputed evidence is never converted to
  absence, completeness or readiness.
- Archive eligibility requires all applicable external evidence and accountable
  approval; local green checks alone cannot close a gate.
