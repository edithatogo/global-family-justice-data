# Code of conduct

The project welcomes respectful participation from researchers, courts, practitioners, public servants, advocates, technologists, and people with lived experience.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and avoid harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct;
- protect confidential and personal information;
- avoid pressuring anyone to disclose family, court, violence, child-protection, or health experiences;
- distinguish factual correction from institutional or personal attack;
- acknowledge uncertainty and methodological disagreement;
- declare material conflicts;
- follow safeguarding and support arrangements for lived-experience participation.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that create safety, privacy, legal, or conduct risks. Serious or repeated breaches may result in restricted participation. Reports should be made privately to the role designated by the institutional host.
