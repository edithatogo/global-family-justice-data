# G2 real-pilot preflight panel — 2026-08-15

Status: advisory preflight; not owner acceptance, methods adjudication, rights
clearance, concordance evidence or publication authority.

Two role-separated analyst agents independently inspected the four preserved,
authentic pilot editions for Australia, Brazil, South Africa and Minnesota.
They were instructed not to inspect one another's work. Because the shared
workspace cannot prove cognitive independence, this is procedural blinding
only.

## Common findings

- All inspected values are aggregate and source-native.
- None is currently suitable for international comparison or gold promotion.
- Australia and Minnesota both publish a measure called clearance, but the
  operational populations and clocks differ.
- Brazil's selected material is a partial-year dynamic-dashboard screenshot
  about protective measures, not general family-court caseflow.
- South Africa discloses incomplete court coverage, contains a 90-day versus
  four-month clock contradiction, and publishes component counts that do not
  sum to the stated total.
- Exact-edition handling, methods disposition and owner adjudication remain
  mandatory.

The two exploratory passes selected different valid rows for Australia and
Minnesota and different cards from the same Brazil figure. They therefore do
not constitute a formal concordance run. This is a useful control finding: the
source-row sample and threshold must be frozen before the formal blinded runs.

## Panel advice

Options for the formal concordance policy are:

1. **Strict critical-field policy (recommended):** require 100% agreement on
   edition, jurisdiction, locator, value, denominator, statistic, unit, clock,
   cohort/population, matter and indicator after documented correction and
   rerun; require at least 99% across all populated fields. No critical
   discrepancy can be waived into a pass.
2. **Uniform 99% policy:** simpler, but can conceal a single material semantic
   disagreement in a small sample.
3. **Qualitative adjudication only:** flexible, but not reproducible and does
   not meet the existing quantitative quality plan.

Recommendation: option 1. If a critical field differs, correct clerical errors
and rerun both paths. If the source itself is ambiguous, preserve the
disagreement and quarantine or exclude the row rather than selecting one
extractor's interpretation. If fewer than four rows remain, report the reduced
scope explicitly and do not infer pilot-wide reliability.

No dissent was recorded. Both agents abstain from accountable acceptance,
rights clearance and publication decisions.
