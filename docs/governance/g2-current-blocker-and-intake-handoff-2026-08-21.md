## Recommended one-time pilot route

Use a **small owner-provided or owner-nominated real pilot cohort** that is not
reused as an unseen holdout. The cohort should contain one exact edition for
each required acquisition route where feasible: API, spreadsheet,
HTML/dashboard and PDF/manual. If a route is unavailable, the campaign must
stop rather than silently substitute it.

Before any acquisition or inspection, the cohort packet must contain only:

1. stable edition identifier, jurisdiction, publisher, series and exact URL or
   local-delivery locator;
2. proposed acquisition route and expected format;
3. a source-specific rights/privacy/disclosure status of `unknown`,
   `local_private`, or `clear`—never inferred from public availability;
4. a candidate-manifest digest, complete exposure-chain check, resource cap and
   stop rules; and
5. the campaign protocol and access-control bindings.

The existing offline validator must reject duplicate or exposure-overlapping
candidate URLs before any campaign packet is frozen.
