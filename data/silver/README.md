# Silver layer

Silver contains normalised long-format records. Dates, IDs, field names, and selected units use controlled representations, while original labels and definitions remain available.

Every silver observation must retain:

- source ID/version and exact provenance;
- original and harmonised measure/matter terms;
- transformation ID or documented manual rule;
- period, cohort, statistic, unit, and denominator;
- review status, quality grade, and comparability tier.

Silver may contain Tier 3 and Tier 4 records for discovery and local analysis. Their limitations must remain explicit.
