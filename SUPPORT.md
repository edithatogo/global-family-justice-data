# Support and corrections

## Supported requests

The project accepts:

- reports of factual or numerical errors;
- missing or superseded official sources;
- broken provenance or download links;
- translation and terminology corrections;
- reproducibility failures;
- accessibility problems;
- proposed jurisdiction profiles, indicators, and methods changes.

Security or potential privacy issues must follow `SECURITY.md`, not a public issue.

## Service model for v1

The institutional host must publish realistic acknowledgement and resolution targets by severity. A suggested model is:

- critical privacy, security, or systemic data-integrity issue: immediate escalation and, where necessary, temporary withdrawal;
- material data error: prompt triage, public issue record, and correction or scoped withdrawal;
- bounded correction or broken source: scheduled maintenance with status updates;
- enhancement or new source: prioritised through the public roadmap.

## Correction principles

- released values are never silently overwritten;
- every correction identifies affected releases, records, and conclusions;
- corrected artifacts receive a new patch release and checksums;
- retractions remain visible with reasons;
- unresolved disputes are documented rather than erased;
- the correction reporter is credited when appropriate and safe.
