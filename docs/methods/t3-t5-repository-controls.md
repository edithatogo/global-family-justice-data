# T3/T5 repository-owned controls

The T3 acquisition path records bytes, checksums, edition identity, source
terms and redistribution routing.  A captured receipt is not evidence of
rights or independent preservation custody.  `gfjd.preservation` therefore
provides a fail-closed readiness check requiring an exact edition, checksum,
authoritative rights disposition, preservation decision reference and custody
receipt reference.  Missing items remain pending and route to the rights or
archive authority; uncertain material remains metadata-only.

T5 comparability audits continue to build exact semantic-signature candidates,
surface definition fragmentation and preserve declared quality/comparability
tiers.  The audit never upgrades a tier, approves pooling, or substitutes for
independent re-extraction and accountable methods adjudication.  Disputed
measures remain quarantined or descriptive-only.
