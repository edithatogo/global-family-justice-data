## Two stages

1. An acquisition custodian, binary-security scanner and structural reviewer
   process candidates in frozen order. The reviewer may emit only bounded
   telemetry, controlled flags and a document-level stratum. Source text,
   excerpts, target values, target locators, screenshots, page images and OCR
   output must not be persisted. Candidate rejection advances to the next frozen
   rank; an authorization, role, digest, sandbox, security or resource violation
   stops the whole lineage.
2. The selector stops at the smallest inspected prefix that deterministically
   supports 24 primaries, six reserves, six primaries per stratum, at least 12
   jurisdictions, no more than two selected editions per jurisdiction and one
   selected edition per source series. Fresh primary and secondary extractors
   receive only the sealed 24-edition bundle and generic contract. They cannot
   see the frame, ranks, rejects, reserves, preflight outputs, earlier packets,
   the other extraction or comparator results.

The exact selector uses the frozen slot order, ascending frame rank and
backtracking tie-break. The required reserve allocation is two raster/dashboard,
two complex mixed-layout, one non-English text-native and one English
text-native edition. The classifier independently recomputes the first matching
stratum under the frozen precedence; an unsafe or inconsistent eligibility
record is rejected.

“Unseen” therefore means unseen by both extraction paths, not uninspected by
every project role. Structural evidence supports only a document-level format
claim, not a claim that the selected target fact itself uses that layout.

## Contracts and stopping rules

- Generic extraction contract: one deterministically selected qualifying
  aggregate fact per edition, explicit-only dates, exact NFC/whitespace
  normalization, 100% concordance for critical fields and at least 99% overall.
- No fuzzy match, critical waiver, post-seal repair, reserve substitution, scope
  shrink, adaptive replenishment or same-lineage rerun.
- No qualifying fact in a sealed primary is an extraction failure, not grounds
  for retrospective exclusion.
- Source bytes and sealed evidence remain controlled local-private; the public
  boundary remains metadata and citation only.
- Every role has an exact artifact allowlist/denylist and must emit a fresh,
  digest-bound access receipt. Structural inspection and extraction are
  distinct roles and sessions. The frozen generic comparator treats every
  identity, source excerpt, empirical field and canonical semantic facet as
  critical; only row-run identity and free notes are ignored.
