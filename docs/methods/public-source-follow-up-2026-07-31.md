# Public-source follow-up after direct-enquiry responses

Checked: 2026-07-31

This is an analyst-agent review of official public sources prompted by the
Swedish National Courts Administration response. It records source leads and
scope statements only. No values are promoted to the observation layer and no
jurisdiction is marked ready without source-edition review, rights review,
coverage assessment, agent validation, and owner adjudication.

## Material official-source findings

### Australia

The Federal Circuit and Family Court annual-report pages publish family-law
filings and finalisations, including final orders, interim orders, divorce
applications, and other applications:
https://www.fcfcoa.gov.au/fcc-annual-reports/2020-21/part-1

### Minnesota

The official dashboard page exposes District Court filings by case type and
performance measures including clearance, time to disposition, and permanency.
Its user guide identifies a Major Family category with selectable subtypes and
district/county/time filters:
https://mncourts.gov/help-topics/court-statistics
https://www.mncourts.gov/mncourtsgov/media/CIOMediaLibrary/Documents/Tableau-Dashboard-User-Guide-Annual-Filings.pdf

### Brazil

CNJ states that DataJud is the primary national judicial-statistics source. Its
statistics page identifies family/child, childhood, domestic-violence, and
protective-measure dashboards, class/subject filters, municipal and judicial-
unit detail, data from 2020, and a public API. The family-and-child dashboard
reports new, pending, judged, and closed cases, performance measures, and
duration:
https://www.cnj.jus.br/programas-e-acoes/estatistica/
https://www.cnj.jus.br/paineis-cnj/
https://datajud-wiki.cnj.jus.br/api-publica/endpoints/

### South Africa

The 2023/24 Annual Judiciary Report includes family-law work such as domestic-
violence and harassment protection orders, children's courts, and maintenance,
but expressly notes incomplete statistics:
https://www.judiciary.org.za/index.php/documents/judiciary-annual-reports?download=23831%3Aannual-judiciary-report-2023-2024

### England and Wales

The accredited Family Court Statistics Quarterly collection covers public and
private law, matrimonial matters, domestic violence, adoption, forced marriage,
and FGM protection orders. Releases include ODS tables, ZIPped CSVs, a guide,
data-quality statement, and visualisation tools. The guide documents live-
database revisions, suppression and unavailable markers. The publication is
licensed under Open Government Licence v3.0 except where otherwise stated:
https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/family-court-statistics-quarterly
https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/family-court-statistics-quarterly-october-to-december-2025
https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/family-court-statistics-quarterly-october-to-december-2025/guide-to-family-court-statistics

### Canada and British Columbia

Statistics Canada publishes a downloadable table, "Family law cases, by type
of case", with divorce, parenting, support, child-protection, civil-protection,
and other family categories:
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3510022201

The 2024/25 release lists participating jurisdictions. It excludes British
Columbia and Ontario from one case-type proportion chart because of inconsistent
labelling and excludes Saskatchewan because of data availability. These are
material completeness/comparability limitations:
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260326/dq260326b-eng.pdf

### Singapore

The Judiciary publishes Family Justice Courts filings, disposals, and clearance
rates, with breakdowns including maintenance and family violence, divorce,
originating summons, probate and summons, and Youth Court:
https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/who-we-are/statistics/caseload-statistics-2023
https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/AnnualReport2022/TT-Statistics.html

### California

The Judicial Council's annual Court Statistics Report provides ten-year
statewide filings and dispositions and current individual-court detail.
Family-law tables distinguish marital/parentage, domestic violence, child
support, and other petitions:
https://courts.ca.gov/news-reference/research-data/court-statistics

### Spain

CGPJ publishes quarterly and annual series by High Court region, province, and
judicial district for annulment, separation, divorce, modification of
matrimonial measures, and custody/support of non-marital children, including an
annual 1981–2024 series:
https://www.poderjudicial.es/cgpj/es/Temas/Estadistica-Judicial/Estadistica-por-temas/Informacion-socioeconomica-relacionada-con-la-Administracion-de-Justicia/Aspectos-socioeconomicos-relacionados-con-la-actividad--judicial/Divorcios--Separaciones-y-Nulidades-ingresados/

### Nuevo León, Mexico

The state judiciary publishes monthly, quarterly, and annual Family Court
statistics. This remains a subnational source and cannot establish Mexican
national coverage:
https://www.pjenl.gob.mx/Estadistica/Juzgados-Familiares/

### Japan

The Supreme Court publishes annual family-matter judicial statistics in PDF and
Excel, including separately posted correction tables. The 2026 Court Data Book
also has family and personal-status case and duration sections:
https://www.courts.go.jp/toukei_siryou/shihotokei_search/list/index.html?filter%5Btype%5D=1&filter%5ByCategory%5D=3&filter%5ByYear%5D=2022
https://www.courts.go.jp/toukei_siryou/databook/index.html

### France

The Ministry of Justice's detailed-statistics service supports CSV export and
offers new cases, completed cases, and average duration by matter and geography.
The 2025 statistical reference includes divorce, post-divorce, parental
authority, residence/contact, and child-maintenance series:
https://www.stats.justice.gouv.fr/chiffres_cles/html/C-CUBE_AFF_NOUV_NAT_AFF
https://www.justice.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2026-01/RSJ2025%20ouvrage%20complet.pdf

### Netherlands

Rechtspraak publishes annual reports and court-level key figures. Public annual
figures distinguish civil-family shares, but the reviewed pages do not yet show
the required detailed family taxonomy:
https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/raad-voor-de-rechtspraak/jaardocumenten
https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/raad-voor-de-rechtspraak/jaardocumenten/jaarverslag-rechtspraak-2024/2024-in-cijfers

### Kenya

The State of the Judiciary report publishes filed, resolved, and clearance
figures by court rank, including Kadhis' Courts. The reviewed result did not
provide the required family-matter breakdown:
https://judiciary.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/SOJAR-2024-2025-Final-Dec-12.pdf

### Chile

The Judiciary's public account publishes first-instance family incoming,
terminated, and pending/in-progress case totals. Separate official material
provides domestic-violence termination statistics from the Family Case
Processing System:
https://cuentapublica.pjud.cl/others/docs/9078.pdf
https://www.pjud.cl/docs/download/38901

### Philippines

The Supreme Court publishes regional caseflow for designated and statutory
Family Courts, including opening pending, new, revived, transferred-in,
decided/resolved, archived, transferred-out, and closing pending counts:
https://sc.judiciary.gov.ph/year-2024/

### India

NJDG provides searchable coverage from Taluka courts through the Supreme Court,
with institution, pendency, disposal, age, case-type, court, and dashboard
views. The reviewed public interface does not yet establish the exact family
taxonomy or download/reuse conditions:
https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in/njdg_v3/

### United States

The NCSC Court Statistics Project publishes annual state-court caseload
dashboards using standardized categories, including Domestic Relations, and
Pending/Incoming/Outgoing statuses. NCSC says the data cover more than 95% of
US filings, which is substantial but not complete national coverage:
https://www.ncsc.org/explore-court-caseload-data
https://www.ncsc.org/our-centers-projects/court-statistics-project
