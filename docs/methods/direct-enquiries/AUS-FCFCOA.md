# Draft enquiry: Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia

Status: **planned; not sent**

## Official route

- Licensing questions: `communication [at] fcfcoa.gov.au`, published on the
  Court's [copyright page](https://www.fcfcoa.gov.au/copyright).
- Research engagement: the
  [Enquiries hub](https://www.fcfcoa.gov.au/enquiries-hub) provides the Court's
  official “Research with the Courts” route.

The copyright page states that website material, unless excluded, is provided
under CC BY-ND 3.0. The sender must ask only about ambiguity outside that
published grant and must re-check the current terms before sending.

## Why an enquiry remains required

The public copyright page substantially resolves ordinary unchanged
redistribution of covered website material, but the repository's assessment is
still partial. It does not establish whether annual-report series provide a
complete and consistently defined family-law time series or whether underlying
aggregate tables are available in reusable form.

## Draft request

Subject: GFJD research enquiry — FCFCOA family-law statistical series

Hello,

I am the analyst-owner of the public, non-commercial Global Family Justice Data
repository. We are mapping aggregate statistics in the FCFCOA annual reports.
Could you please confirm:

1. the authoritative annual-report tables for filings, finalisations,
   pending workload, clearance, and elapsed time by family-law matter type;
2. any definition or structural breaks associated with the September 2021
   court reforms and the recommended method for a historical series;
3. whether machine-readable versions or underlying aggregate tables are
   publicly available; and
4. whether the Court's published CC BY-ND 3.0 terms cover unchanged report
   tables and metadata, and what permission is required for transformed
   derived observations.

We will preserve attribution and provenance and will not publish case-level
information.

Regards,

[repository owner]
